## Methods Context

- Fisher exact testing is used for sparse 2 x 2 gene-response tables.
- Benjamini-Hochberg q-values are used to control false discovery rate across tested genes.
- Tumor mutational burden, neoantigen load, and immune-context estimates are interpreted as exploratory translational features, not standalone clinical decision rules.
